Concepts & Questions

A working space for veterinary questions that deserve to be defined before they become claims. We use source-linked evidence to identify clinical and system constraints, test assumptions and develop research hypotheses around oxygen delivery across species.

Evidence boundary: concepts published here are not established efficacy claims, regulatory approvals or clinical guidance. The underlying publications, regulatory records, documented cases and real-world evidence remain in the separate BHOC-platform evidence library and are linked from each concept.
Current concept

Questions worth investigating.

Donor welfare · Cross-species access

Do we have the right to depend on healthy animal donors?

Blood donation saves lives, but donor dependence changes dramatically across species. Cats frequently require sedation. Horses have exceptionally complex red-cell compatibility. Camel transfusion systems remain incompletely characterized. For birds, wildlife, endangered species and cetaceans, a practical compatible donor may not exist when the emergency occurs. What should the next layer of oxygen-delivery support look like?
