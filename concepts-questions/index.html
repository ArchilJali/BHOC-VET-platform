Concepts & Questions

A working space for veterinary questions that deserve to be defined before they become claims. The purpose is to connect real clinical constraints with source-linked evidence, test assumptions and develop stronger research hypotheses around oxygen delivery.

Evidence boundary: concepts published here are not established efficacy claims, regulatory approvals or clinical guidance. Supporting data remain traceable to original publications, regulatory records and the separate Vet Real-World Evidence & Cases library.
Current concept

Questions worth investigating.

Donor welfare · transfusion access

Do we have the right to depend on healthy animal donors?

Veterinary blood donation saves lives. But dogs and cats cannot provide informed consent, feline donation frequently requires chemical restraint, and emergency access still depends on donor availability, compatibility and location. If a donor-independent oxygen-delivery layer could reduce that dependence, should veterinary medicine investigate it?
