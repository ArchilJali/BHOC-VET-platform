# BHOC VET-platform

**Veterinary Direction · Knowledge Base**

Guided veterinary research portal for oxygen biology, regulatory context, concepts and scientific publications.

## Public interface

- [Open BHOC VET-platform](https://archiljali.github.io/BHOC-VET-platform/)

## BHOC ecosystem

### Websites

- [BHOC Therapeutics](https://bhoctherapeutics.com/) — main BHOC website
- [BHOC Veterinary](https://bhocvet.com/) — veterinary website, in `BHOC-VET-Therapeutics`
- [BHOC Transplant](https://bhoctransplant.com/) — transplant website, in `BHOC-Transplant-Therapeutics`

### Knowledge bases and evidence platforms

- [BHOC Therapeutics Platform](https://archiljali.github.io/BHOC-platform/) — central history, historical HBOC catalogues and shared source records
- **BHOC VET-platform** — veterinary direction knowledge base, this repository; new veterinary research records belong here as they are reviewed and published
- [Historical Veterinary Evidence Library](https://archiljali.github.io/BHOC-platform/veterinary/Vet-index.html) — source-linked historical veterinary publications and regulatory records kept in the central platform
- [BHOC Transplant evidence platform](https://archiljali.github.io/BHOC-Transplant-platform/) — current transplant-related research context and methodology, separate from the central historical transplant catalogue
- [Real-World Evidence](https://archiljali.github.io/BHOC-platform/real-world-evidence/) — public Oxygen Delivery Evidence knowledge-base entry

Public ecosystem navigation should point to the public interfaces above. Historical catalogues remain in the central platform; new direction-specific evidence can develop in the dedicated platform without duplicating an editable historical database.

BHOC means Biological Hemoglobin Oxygen Carrier.
